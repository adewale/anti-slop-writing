Verdict: revise
Slop tells: "found every critical vulnerability," "understood our threat model," and "gave the security team confidence" turn a security update into certainty and morale without evidence.
Specificity missing: Ask the author which scanner was used, what vulnerability classes it covered, what "critical" means, how findings were verified, and whether patches were generated, reviewed, or only suggested.
Inflated claim: "Every" and "understood" overstate coverage and agency unless the team can show scan scope, verification, and review records.
Flow break: The sentence moves from detection to patch generation to confidence without naming the handoff or validation step.
Concrete rewrite: Until those details are sourced: The scanner found critical vulnerabilities before launch, and the security team reviewed the fixes before shipping.
Remembered line: Security prose should name coverage before it claims certainty.
