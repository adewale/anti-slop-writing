## Self-score gate (candidate, from stop-slop)

After drafting or rewriting, score the output 1-10 on five axes, then act on the total:

```txt
Directness: states, or announces? ("this underscores," "it is worth noting" score low)
Rhythm:     varied sentence length, or metronomic?
Reader trust: facts stated plainly, without hedging or over-justification?
Authenticity: would a domain practitioner write this line, or is it essay voice?
Density:    is anything cuttable without losing a named mechanism?
```

Score each axis by whether a named mechanism, actor, or concrete carrier is present, not by surface tokens. A passive verb, em-dash, or adverb that carries a real relation scores high; do not dock points for tokens alone.

If the total is below 35/50, revise the lowest axis once and rescore. Do not revise a line that already names its mechanism merely to raise a token-level score.

## Cut quotables (candidate, from stop-slop)

Flag any line engineered to read like a pull-quote: a self-contained aphorism whose force comes from cadence rather than from the sentence before it. Apply the emphasis-source test — flatten the line; if the residual claim still names an actor, mechanism, or limit, keep it; if it collapses into a generic statement, the cadence was carrying it, so rewrite to name the mechanism. Do not flag a quotable line whose claim survives flattening.
