Verdict: revise

Slop tells: Three hollow modifiers in three consecutive sentences — "My actual recommendation," "The real reason," "The key issue." Each one implies a contrast the document cannot supply. "Actual" implies an earlier, non-actual recommendation; "real" implies a stated reason that was fake; "key" implies a ranked field of lesser issues. Nothing precedes this paragraph, so all three contrasts point at prose that does not exist. That is false implicature, not emphasis. The paragraph also runs three identical noun-phrase-plus-copula openers in a row ("My X is to...," "The X is that...," "The X is..."), which is symmetrical cadence standing in for structure.

Specificity missing: The first and third sentences carry none. The eleven-hour unpausable backfill is the only concrete detail in the paragraph, and it arrives buried behind "The real reason is that." Sentence three names an open decision but describes it as a "key issue" rather than saying what its status is — unassigned, disputed, or blocking the ship date. "Key" ranks; it does not report.

Inflated claim: "actual," "real," and "key" all claim salience by contrast rather than by evidence. Apply the deletion test: strike all three and the paragraph loses no information. A modifier that can be deleted without loss was decorative. The correction is deletion, not expansion — replacing "the real reason" with a longer justification for why this reason is the real one would keep the phantom contrast and add words to it.

Flow break: The three sentences sit side by side in a plausible order with no relation named between them. The backfill is not a second fact after the recommendation; it is the cause of it. The rollback owner is not a third parallel item; it is an unresolved dependency of the same recommendation, because a flag is only useful if someone is authorized to flip it back. Subordination should carry both relations instead of leaving the reader to infer them from sentence order.

Concrete rewrite:
"Ship the migration behind a flag, because the backfill takes eleven hours and cannot be paused once it starts. Who owns the rollback is still undecided."

Rewrite check: passes self-detectors. No rule-of-three, no X-not-Y, no em-dash antithesis, no avoid-by-default phrases, no prestige adjectives, no decorative closure. The three hollow modifiers are deleted rather than swapped for synonyms ("genuine," "primary," "central" would repeat the failure under new vocabulary). The rewrite invents no name, count, tool, or timing: eleven hours, the unpausable backfill, the flag, and the undecided rollback owner all come from the source. Note on the imperative: "Ship the migration behind a flag" keeps the recommendation without the possessive frame; if the doc's convention requires attribution, "I recommend shipping the migration behind a flag" is the same sentence with the modifier still gone.
