Verdict: revise

Slop tells: Three hollow modifiers stacked in a single sentence — "true benefit," "genuine visibility," "actual state." Each asserts authenticity by implying a counterfeit alternative: a false benefit claimed earlier, fake visibility some other scheduler provides, a job state that is not the actual one. The sentence supplies none of those contrasts, and nothing in it can. "The X of Y is that..." is also a copula frame that demotes the only working verb ("gives") into a subordinate clause, so the scheduler reaches the subject position fourth.

Specificity missing: "visibility into the state of every job" names no state and no surface. Queued, running, retrying, failed, blocked on a lock? Read where — a status endpoint, a dashboard, a log line, an API field? The sentence answers neither, and the source line supplies nothing that would let me answer for it.

Inflated claim: "true" and "genuine" are the claim. Strip them and the sentence loses no information, which is the test for a decorative modifier. Leave them in and they promise a comparison the writer never makes. The fix is to delete them, not to expand them: writing a clause that explains why this benefit is the true one preserves the phantom contrast and pays extra words for it. The same applies to "actual" — swapping in "real-time" or "current" would look like a fix while inventing a timing claim the source never made.

Flow break: Not applicable to a single line, but the deletion has a downstream consequence worth naming. Once the modifiers are gone, the sentence is honest and thin, which makes it obvious that the next sentence has to carry the mechanism. That is the correct place for the work.

Concrete rewrite:
"The new scheduler shows the state of every job."

Rewrite check: passes self-detectors. No rule-of-three, no X-not-Y, no em-dash antithesis, no banned phrases, no prestige adjectives, no decorative closure, no invented facts. The three modifiers are deleted rather than replaced with synonyms, and the nominalization "visibility" collapses back into the verb "shows." The rewrite deliberately stops short of naming which states or which surface, because the source supplies neither. If the line has to earn "visibility" on its own, ask the author: which job states does the scheduler expose, and where does a team read them? Fallback if that answer is not available: keep the short line above and let the following sentence name the states and the surface.
