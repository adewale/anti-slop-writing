"The billing job runs ledger-fold passes after each import, so invoice-drift totals stay accurate. Each pass finishes before the nightly export starts, and the export reads only folded rows."

**Verdict:** ask-author

**Slop tells:** None of the standard cadence tells are present — no banned phrases, no prestige adjectives, no copula displacement, no hedged symmetry, no em-dash antithesis, no rule-of-three. The real issue is a coined compound label: "ledger-fold" names a process the paragraph never defines (what does a fold actually change about a row — dedupe? correction? aggregation?), and the hyphen makes it sound precise without making it checkable. By contrast, "invoice-drift totals" is fine as written: it names the metric being tracked, not a mechanism, so it doesn't need a definition the way "fold" does (false-positive restraint: a metric label isn't the same kind of claim as a mechanism name).

**Specificity missing:** What a fold pass actually does to a row. The paragraph gives fold's timing (after each import, before the export) and its output classification (rows become "folded") but never its transformation — so "accurate" has no visible mechanism under it.

**Inflated claim:** "so invoice-drift totals stay accurate" claims correctness. The only support given — the pass finishes before the export starts, and the export reads only folded rows — proves a consistency property (the export never reads a half-folded batch, mixing pre- and post-fold rows), not a correctness property (that the folded values are arithmetically right). Consistency and accuracy are not the same claim, and the paragraph moves from one to the other without marking the move. The claim is also unbounded: no stated behavior for a fold pass that fails or is skipped, or a correction that lands after the nightly export has already run.

**Flow break:** Sentence 1 asserts a result ("so ... stay accurate"); sentence 2 reads as its justification but never says so — it's joined with "and," not "because," and it never states that "the export" is the same process that produces the invoice-drift totals from sentence 1. The reader has to supply both the causal link and the identity link himself.

**Concrete rewrite:**
Ask author: what does a ledger-fold pass change about a ledger row — dedupe re-imported lines, apply a reversal or correction, aggregate line items into the total? And does "accurate" mean the fold's arithmetic is correct, or only that the export never reads a stale, half-folded batch? That fact decides whether the claim is earned by a correction mechanism or is really just an ordering guarantee wearing a stronger word.
